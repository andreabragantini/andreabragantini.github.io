# Analysis Results Visualizations

This directory contains the visual outputs generated by the Germany 2025 Power Market analysis. The plots are organized into two subdirectories based on their content:

## 📊 1. Demand Visualizations (`results/demand/`)
This folder contains time-series analysis and profiles of electricity demand in Germany for 2025.

* **[demand_cleaned.png](file:///c:/Users/Pc/Documents/GitHub/power-market-data-analysis/results/demand/demand_cleaned.png)**: The final cleaned and imputed demand time-series, where outliers were replaced and gaps were filled using seasonal imputation.
* **[demand_distribution.png](file:///c:/Users/Pc/Documents/GitHub/power-market-data-analysis/results/demand/demand_distribution.png)**: A histogram and boxplot illustrating the distribution of electricity demand values (MWh/h).
* **[intra_day_profile.png](file:///c:/Users/Pc/Documents/GitHub/power-market-data-analysis/results/demand/intra_day_profile.png)**: The average 24-hour electricity demand pattern across Germany.
* **[intra_day_profile_by_weekday.png](file:///c:/Users/Pc/Documents/GitHub/power-market-data-analysis/results/demand/intra_day_profile_by_weekday.png)**: Hourly demand patterns split by day of the week (showing lower demand during weekends).
* **[monthly_profiles.png](file:///c:/Users/Pc/Documents/GitHub/power-market-data-analysis/results/demand/monthly_profiles.png)**: A 4-panel comparison (Total Demand, Peak Load, Base Load, Volatility) for each month.
* **[mstl_decomposition.png](file:///c:/Users/Pc/Documents/GitHub/power-market-data-analysis/results/demand/mstl_decomposition.png)**: Multiple Seasonal-Trend decomposition using LOESS showing the Trend, Daily Seasonality, Weekly Seasonality, and Residual components of the demand.

## ⚡ 2. Price Visualizations (`results/price/`)
This folder contains plots exploring day-ahead pricing dynamics and price-demand relationships.

* **[hourly_power_price.png](file:///c:/Users/Pc/Documents/GitHub/power-market-data-analysis/results/price/hourly_power_price.png)**: The day-ahead hourly electricity price across 2025, highlighting the yearly average price line (89.32 EUR/MWh) and the 0 EUR/MWh threshold.
* **[intra_day_profile.png](file:///c:/Users/Pc/Documents/GitHub/power-market-data-analysis/results/price/intra_day_profile.png)**: The average 24-hour price profile.
* **[intra_day_profile_by_weekday.png](file:///c:/Users/Pc/Documents/GitHub/power-market-data-analysis/results/price/intra_day_profile_by_weekday.png)**: Daily price curves split by day of the week.
* **[intra_day_profile_by_season.png](file:///c:/Users/Pc/Documents/GitHub/power-market-data-analysis/results/price/intra_day_profile_by_season.png)**: A comparison of daily price curves between Winter (Oct-Mar) and Summer (Apr-Sep).
* **[daily_price_vs_demand.png](file:///c:/Users/Pc/Documents/GitHub/power-market-data-analysis/results/price/daily_price_vs_demand.png)**: Dual-axis plot of daily average price vs. total daily demand.
* **[price_vs_demand_scatter_hourly.png](file:///c:/Users/Pc/Documents/GitHub/power-market-data-analysis/results/price/price_vs_demand_scatter_hourly.png)**: Scatter plot showing the correlation between price and demand on an hourly basis.
* **[price_vs_demand_scatter_before0110.png](file:///c:/Users/Pc/Documents/GitHub/power-market-data-analysis/results/price/price_vs_demand_scatter_before0110.png)**: Scatter plot of price vs. demand before October 1st, 2025 (operated on hourly granularity).
* **[price_vs_demand_scatter_after0110.png](file:///c:/Users/Pc/Documents/GitHub/power-market-data-analysis/results/price/price_vs_demand_scatter_after0110.png)**: Scatter plot of price vs. demand after October 1st, 2025 (operated on 15-minute granularity).
